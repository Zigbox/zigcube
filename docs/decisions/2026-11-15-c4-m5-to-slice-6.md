# M5 and its tooth move to C4 slice 6

Date: 2026-11-15. Asked by the C4 slice 5 implementer; agreed by the owner (via the supervisor)
the same day.

The brief puts sim-c4's M5 ("during a rollout with no node fault, a simulated client at constant
load sees no failed request") and its `mesh_no_drain` tooth in slice 5, but the drain they judge
(a replica leaves the endpoints and finishes its connections before it stops) is slice 6's work:
the agent does not wait for a drain yet. Checking M5 before the drain exists would either fail
every rollout or need a check weakened until slice 6.

Decision: M5, `mesh_no_drain` and the agent's drain wait all land in slice 6, next to the drain.
Slice 5 checks M1, M2 and M4. `mesh_revoke_ms` is 1,000 ms (`src/constants.zig`), as owner
answer O9 already approved. The cut is recorded in `docs/demos/c4.md`.
