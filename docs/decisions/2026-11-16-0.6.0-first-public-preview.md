# 0.6.0 is the first public preview, after a full review of each product

*Decided 2026-11-16, by the owner.*

Zigbox 0.6.0 and Zigcube 0.6.0 are the project's first releases offered to the public as products
people may rely on. They are labelled **preview**. Every release before 0.6.0 was a working
milestone, not a public offer.

Because of that, each product gets a top-to-bottom review before its 0.6.0 release. This review
comes on top of the two independent release reviews AGENTS.md "Releases" asks for, and does not
replace them. It reads the whole product as it stands, not the diff since the last release, and it
covers three things:

1. **Design issues.** Interfaces, records, defaults, limits and error codes that would be costly to
   change once people depend on them.
2. **Security.** The whole attack surface, not only what changed since the last release.
3. **Testing improvements.** Gaps in the unit, simulation, fuzz and VM tests, and tests that
   cannot fail.

It is run with the most capable model available at the time. Its findings are fixed, or the owner
accepts them in writing in `docs/decisions/`, before the 0.6.0 tag.

**Zigcube:** the review runs before the Jepsen battery and the 0.6.0 simulation soak, because
fixing its findings can change what those test. Jepsen and the soak then run on a commit that
already includes the fixes.

**Zigbox:** 0.6.0 is S2 (lazy start). The review runs once S2 is complete on `main`, and before
the 0.6.0 release reviews.

The same decision is recorded in Zigbox as
`docs/decisions/2026-11-16-0.6.0-first-public-preview.md`.
