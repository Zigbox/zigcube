# Agent: a stop whose grace has passed is not a wake time (issue 96)

*Decided 2026-11-05, by the fix for issue 96.*

`Agent.next_wake` counts a pending stop's deadline only while it is in the future. The busy-loop ceiling (200 steps per minute of uptime, `sim_cluster.zig`) is unchanged: the agent really spun, so the agent is fixed and the check's budget is not.

**Evidence.** Seed 2230515380 (shrunk to 85 operations, `crash_heavy`, 2 nodes, ttl 8,516 ms, 200 % replicas, heal at 72). Node 0 took 1,025 steps in 296.9 s (207 a minute); its steps are one a second except two bursts of 4 steps per 100 ms tick for about 6 s (206 and 164 to 209 steps in a 10 s window). A print in `next_wake` showed `delta-26`'s pending stop overdue (deadline 851,612 ms) on every step of a burst, and the kill (`stop` with grace 0) being issued each step with the container still `starting` afterwards. The cause: the container was an orphan whose record had been written and whose start never ran (the agent crashed between the two), so `SimNode` (and `FakeRuntime`) report it `starting` until `fake_start_timeout_ms` (10 s) and there is no process to kill. The stop is therefore pending past its 5 s grace, and `next_wake` returned `deadline < now`, that is now, until the record turned `lost`. The same happens on a real node: any stop that cannot end its container within the grace made the agent spin until it did.

**Fix.** An overdue stop has been acted on in the pass that computed the wake (`orphan_containers` and `replace_stale` call `stop_once` on every pending stop whose container is still listed; the others are forgotten), so only a deadline in the future shortens the sleep. A container that survives the kill is seen again by the resync (`agent_resync_ms`, 1 s). Regression test: `src/sim_cluster_main.zig`, "a stop past its grace over a record that never started does not make a busy loop (issue 96)" (the pinned seed; it failed on main with the ceiling's message).

**Issue 78 (refencing at low lease ttls) does not apply.** The ttl is 8,516 ms, above the 8,000 ms below which `crash_heavy` runs were expected to fail, and the run fenced 3 and 2 times in 300 s, nowhere near a refencing loop. The earlier reports in `2026-11-04-fleet-profiles-and-shapes.md` (seeds 254 and 343, ttls 5,000 to 6,200 ms) were attributed to #78 from the check's symptom alone. The review of this fix replayed them (`sim_cluster 254 378 3 --profile=crash_heavy --lease-ttl-ms=5181 --replicas-pct=300 --heal-at=347` and `sim_cluster 343 736 1 --profile=crash_heavy --lease-ttl-ms=5129 --replicas-pct=100 --heal-at=700`): both fail the busy-loop check identically with and without this fix (byte-identical reports), so they are not this bug and stay with #78. The auto-drawn seeds 478, 551, 1468, 1698 and 1841 no longer reproduce on either build, so they say nothing either way.

Where: `src/agent.zig`, `src/sim_cluster_main.zig`. Related: `2026-10-31-agent-a-pending-stop-is-dropped.md` (the other way a pending stop kept the agent awake).
