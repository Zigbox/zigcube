# C1 slice 4 (4a, 4b, 4e, 4f; the auditor and repair, 4c and 4d, are in the row above)

*Decided 2026-11-02.*

C1 slice 4 (4a, 4b, 4e, 4f; the auditor and repair, 4c and 4d, are in the row above). **A lost node's replicas get new ids** (owner): past `lost_grace_ms` the replica controller writes `mark_replica_lost` (cause the node's `mark_lost`) and replaces the replica under a new id; the scheduler releases the old assignment and never moves it (`move_replica` stays, uncalled, for moves that keep an id). The replica controller reads `/assigned/` and `/node-status/` for this, an exception to authority.md §2 reported to the owner. **No lease-contract change** (owner): a store outage longer than the ttl ends the lease and the node is lost. **A node seen registered again after its old lease ended** is written `mark_lost` then `mark_ready` in one transaction, so the order of the server's expiry and the agent's re-registration does not change the decisions. **Observed removal waits the grace** too. **`zcube-server --lost-grace-ms N`** (file key `lost_grace_ms`, at most an hour, else ZC-E0106) sets both controllers; `demo-c0` runs with 30 s and an 11 s store outage (`docs/demos/c0.md`). **Summaries are written in `controller.put_decision`** (3 ops and 1 compare per decision; batch bounds re-derived: node controller 2 nodes per batch). Repair runs in the request, by the scheduler (the row above).
