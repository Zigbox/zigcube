# C1 slice 4 (4c, 4d)

*Decided 2026-11-02.*

C1 slice 4 (4c, 4d). **The allocation auditor** (`src/auditor.zig`, role `auditor`, actor `allocation-auditor`) runs after the scheduler on every control-plane step, rebuilds each node's tally from `/assigned/<node>/` (`src/allocations.zig`, shared with the scheduler's own check) and opens `/audit/allocations/<node>` with an `allocation_mismatch` decision carrying `ZC-B0003`, or deletes it with `audit_clear`; it never repairs. **The repair runs in the request**: `POST /v1/repair/allocations/{node}?confirm=true` calls the scheduler's `repair` (role and actor `scheduler`) in the API handler, compares the record and the finding, retries a conflict three times (`ZC-E0403`), and needs an open finding (`ZC-E0402`) and `confirm` (`ZC-E0401`, which says what it would write). The CLI's check fails with `ZC-E0404` (exit 1) on a disagreement. New codes use `04xx` so as not to meet slice 7's `01xx`. `GET /v1/status` gains `findings` (additive). A `POST` with no length has no body (RFC 9112 6.3).
