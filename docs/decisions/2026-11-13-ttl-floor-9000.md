# The shortest lease ttl the server admits is 9,000 ms

*Decided 2026-11-13, by the supervisor (the owner accepted #238's recommendations).*

The minimum lease ttl stays at **9,000 ms**, refused below with ZC-E0121. It is
`fence_margin_ms` (4,000 ms) plus `fence_window_min_ms` (5,000 ms). The margin grew from
3,000 ms by two terms of #238: 500 ms for a forward step of the store's clock (finding 3) and
500 ms for how late an agent's fence or kill step may run (finding 4). The window was kept at
5,000 ms rather than cut to 4,000 to keep the renewal room #78 was about.

The owner accepted #238's recommendations 2(a) (a `/node-tenure/<node>` key, so a tenure that
ended unjudged gets a `lost` record and a grace of its own), 3(a) (the clock-step term) and 4(a)
(the step-lag term, with the agent killing at once past it), with 1 s of slack in the margin.

This supersedes the 8,000 ms floor of `2026-11-06-renewal-before-fence.md`. A server configured
with `--lease-ttl-ms` from 8,000 to 8,999 is refused at start; the upgrade order is in
`2026-11-13-238-authority-repairs.md`.
