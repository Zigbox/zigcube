Slice 10c, gaps closed: (1) `test-store-faults -Dbackend=postgres` also runs the whole conformance suite (`store_conformance.zig`, 104 tests) with every connection through a fault proxy (`ZCUBE_TEST_PROXY`, `store_harness.zig`) that adds latency, bandwidth limits and one-way stalls, healed between turns. It never cuts or black-holes: the suite's tests assert exact outcomes (all racing creates but one lose, every increment lands, a watch sees every revision once), so a cut connection would make an error that is a correct response to the fault, not a bug; cuts, lost acknowledgements and partitions are the history runner's job, whose checker allows for them. The single-threaded half runs through the proxy too, which costs time (about a minute) and checks nothing more. (2) The runner issues compare-and-deletes and, in a stage 2 after the log is read, historical reads and compactions. Stage 2 is separate because a compacted log cannot be read back; `check_compaction` judges its reads against the log read in stage 1. Delete is a register reset above a revision floor; a delete's success branch carries an audit put so a lost answer is found in the log. New lies: `keep_delete` (`event_mismatch`), `ignore_compact` (`compacted_read_served`), `over_compact` (`read_above_compaction_failed`). Not done: leases (grant, revoke, expiry on attached keys); the store's lease clock is injected in the suite and real elsewhere, so a model needs a time source the checker does not have yet.

Where: `src/store_faults.zig`, `src/store_harness.zig`, `src/testing/history.zig`, `src/store_mutant.zig`, `build.zig`
