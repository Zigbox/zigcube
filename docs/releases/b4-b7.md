## What needs the Zigbox v0.5.1 pin

Zigbox v0.5.1 is not tagged yet: its release workflow failed on a missing repository variable. Until
it is, the pin stays at Zigbox main aa4d64c (0.5.0 plus `zigbox.trust`, #124).

The steps once the tag exists:

- [ ] **Change the pin.** In `build.zig.zon`, set the `zigbox` URL and `.hash` to the v0.5.1
  tarball, and update its comment's pin history.
- [ ] **Check B5's trust surface against the tag.**
  - The surface is `zigbox.trust`: `job_trust.judge_run`, the policy parser, and the signature
    envelopes behind ZBX-E0905 and ZBX-E0906.
  - It is the only reason the pin is off a tag today.
- [ ] **Check the version demo-b3 and demo-b5 use in the guest.**
  - The guest's `zbx` (`zbx sign`, `zbx trust export`) and the `zigbox` runtime of
    `zcube-agent --runtime zigbox` must come from v0.5.1.
  - Both demos run only on the KVM host in CI.
- [ ] **Run the shared diagnostics against the tag.**
  - Zigbox is canonical for DIAGNOSTICS.md and the shared `diagnostic`, `product`, `render` and
    `explain` modules.
  - Rerun `zig build errors-site` and the tidy-checked code registry against the tag.
- [ ] **Confirm CI's Zigbox fetch works at the tag.** CI fetches the pinned tarball.
- [ ] **Rerun every gate after the pin change.** That means `zig build check`, the demos and the
  sweeps, on the new commit.

## Open items before the tag

1. **The `b3` tag comes first.** Zigcube PRs are held until it exists, and the reviewers' diff
   starts from it.
2. **Five slices are still to merge.** They are listed above, and each is merged as it is cleared.
   The checklist gates restart on the commit after the last of them.
3. **The run-certificate endpoint under the `admin` scope.** Should it be node-only, like the
   credential endpoint? (Integration record, item 1.)
4. **`pem_anchor` (B4) against B7's PEM module.** A duplicate to fold or keep. (Integration
   record, item 2.)
5. **Fleet canaries.** The canaries for B4's teeth and `job_pass_break` were measured before B5 and
   B6 changed the worlds. Confirm their rates on the bundle commit before the soak counts.
   `job_pass_break` is reseeded already: 225 and 180, since 44 no longer fires
   (`docs/decisions/2026-11-14-bundle-teeth-reseed.md`).
6. **A flaky store_faults test.** `store_faults`'s self-test ("rejects each kind of lie with its
   own verdict") once reported `read_at_wrong` for the `ignore_compact` lie. It then passed 12 runs
   in a row. No branch in the bundle touches it, so it looks like a thread-timing flake, but it is
   not yet filed.
