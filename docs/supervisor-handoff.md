*Updated 2026-11-18 by the supervisor session that shipped Zigbox 0.5.3 and Zigcube 0.5.2, for the

The owner decides; the supervisor runs the work. Both repos' `AGENTS.md` bind you; read them first
(Zigbox's `AGENTS.md`, `REVIEW.md` and `DIAGNOSTICS.md` are where new rules show up first).

## Role

- Run each slice as: a fresh implementer session (`create_session`, `source_url`, full 40-char
  `source_revision`, `extra_allowed_tools ["mcp__claude-code-remote__add_repo"]`), then two
  independent review sessions that post `COMMENT` reviews on the PR, then one fresh fix session,
  then a squash merge on green. Confirm with `get_session` that each new session is running.
- **At most two working sessions at once** (owner: "2 models at a time"). Reviews and Haiku
  babysitters (fuzz shards) may overlap. Mechanical work goes to Haiku.
- Archive every session when it finishes.
- Make sane assumptions, record them in the log and the PR, and ping the owner only when blocked.
  Owner reports are 15 lines or fewer. Instructions to the owner are full-stack and CLI-first,
  ideally one re-runnable script (AGENTS.md, owner 2026-11-17).
- Rerun a flaky CI job once at most. No sleep polling: use `send_later`.
- The owner cuts and approves tags (the `release` environment in `release.yml`).

## Mechanics

- **Commits:** trailers `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>` and
  `Claude-Session: <your session URL>`; Zigcube commits are signed off (`git commit -s`). No model
  name anywhere else in a repo. Status-only commits carry `[skip ci]`.
- **Merges:** squash, with your own title and message and the full SHA as `expectedHeadSha`.
  Zigcube and zigcube-jepsen merge messages end with
  `Signed-off-by: topwombat <229780814+topwombat@users.noreply.github.com>` with raw angle
  brackets (zigcube-jepsen's `50eebd3` has an HTML-escaped one; it cannot be fixed).
- **GitHub:** writes go through the MCP tools; `gh api` is for REST reads only. No force-push, no
  rebase. Push only to your session branch, `vm/*` and `release-test/**`. PR bodies end with the
  Claude Code footer and session link; comments end with the "Generated by Claude Code" footer.
- **Secrets:** never print, log or put on argv any token, key, passphrase, the App `.pem`, Apple
  credentials or the BetterStack heartbeat URL. GCP access is WIF only, no key files.
- To read a child's report: `list_events` with `kinds ["result"]`.
